How a query is answered

The same pipeline runs on every document at startup and on your query at search time.

  1. 1Dataset{{ stats.n_docs|thousands }} research titles and abstracts
  2. 2PreprocessingCase folding, cleaning, tokenizing, stopwords, stemming
  3. 3IndexingInverted index from term to documents
  4. 4TF-IDFw = tf × log10(N / df)
  5. 5VSMCosine similarity between query and document
  6. 6RankingHighest score first; zero scores are dropped
